Crab Omelet with Rice

Inspired by: rice, egg, salt, crab
Real-world version: crab fried rice under a soft omelet
Serves: 2 · Time: 25 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice, chilled if possible
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 oz crab meat
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 scallion, sliced
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Stir-fry rice in butter until hot and lightly toasted.
  2. Fold in crab, soy sauce, scallion, salt, and pepper.
  3. Beat eggs with a pinch of salt.
  4. Cook eggs in a nonstick pan into two soft omelets.
  5. Slide each omelet over a mound of crab rice.

Rustic and unfussy — the way camp food should be.

Crab Risotto

Inspired by: rice, butter, rock salt, crab
Real-world version: lemon-butter crab risotto
Serves: 4 · Time: 35 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 cup arborio rice
  • 6 oz crab meat
  • 4 cups warm seafood or chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp butter, plus 1 tbsp to finish
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 1/2 cup white wine or extra stock
  • 1/4 cup parmesan
  • Lemon zest, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Sauté shallot in butter. Add rice and toast 2 minutes.
  2. Add wine and stir until mostly absorbed.
  3. Add warm stock a ladle at a time, stirring until rice is creamy and tender.
  4. Fold in crab, parmesan, final butter, lemon zest, salt, and pepper.
  5. Serve immediately; risotto waits for no hero.

Stir it patiently; risotto waits for no hero.

Curry Pilaf

Inspired by: rice, butter, egg, spice
Real-world version: golden curry rice pilaf
Serves: 4 · Time: 35 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups rice
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 2 1/4 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 boiled eggs or fried eggs, to serve

Method

  1. Rinse rice until the water runs mostly clear.
  2. Cook onion in butter, then stir in curry powder and rice.
  3. Add stock and salt. Cover and simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Rest 10 minutes off heat, then fluff.
  5. Serve with sliced boiled eggs or a fried egg.

Keep this one in the back pocket.

Curry Rice

Inspired by: rice and Goron spice
Real-world version: Japanese-style curry rice
Serves: 4 · Time: 45 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 1 potato, chopped
  • 2 cups stock or water
  • 2 blocks Japanese curry roux, or 2 tbsp curry powder plus 1 tbsp flour
  • Optional: peas, chicken, or tofu

Method

  1. Cook onion in oil until golden.
  2. Add carrots, potato, optional protein, and stock. Simmer until tender.
  3. Stir in curry roux until glossy and thick.
  4. Spoon over hot rice.

The leftovers are arguably better the next day.

Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl

Inspired by: gourmet meat, rice, rock salt
Real-world version: steak rice bowl with pan sauce
Serves: 2 · Time: 30 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 cups hot cooked rice
  • 12 oz steak
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 scallion, sliced
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season steak generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Sear in oil to desired doneness, then rest.
  3. Add butter and soy sauce to the hot pan.
  4. Slice steak over rice and spoon pan sauce on top.
  5. Finish with scallion.

Worth making once and then again by memory.

Inspired by: rice, butter, salt, mushrooms
Real-world version: classic mushroom risotto
Serves: 4 · Time: 40 min · Difficulty: Medium

  • 12 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 1/2 cup white wine or extra stock
  • 4 cups warm stock
  • 1/4 cup parmesan
  • Salt and pepper

Method
