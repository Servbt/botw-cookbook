Apple Pie

Inspired by: Tabantha wheat, cane sugar, goat butter, apple
Real-world version: buttery apple table pie
Serves: 6 slices · Time: 1 hr 20 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 double pie crust, homemade or store-bought
  • 5 medium apples, peeled and sliced
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp white sugar
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp butter, diced
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing

Method

  1. Heat oven to 400°F / 200°C.
  2. Toss apples with sugars, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt.
  3. Line a pie dish with crust, fill with apples, dot with butter, and cover with the second crust.
  4. Crimp, vent, brush with egg, and bake 45–55 minutes until deeply golden.
  5. Rest 20 minutes before slicing so the filling sets.

Best eaten warm after a rainy ride between stables.

Carrot Cake

Inspired by: wheat, cane sugar, goat butter, carrot
Real-world version: small spiced carrot snack cake
Serves: 8 squares · Time: 55 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup melted butter or neutral oil
  • 1 1/2 cups grated carrot
  • Optional: 1/3 cup walnuts or raisins

Method

  1. Heat oven to 350°F / 175°C and line an 8-inch pan.
  2. Whisk dry ingredients in one bowl and eggs, sugar, and butter in another.
  3. Fold wet into dry, then stir in carrot and optional nuts or raisins.
  4. Bake 28–35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  5. Cool before slicing. Add cream cheese frosting if you want the stable-inn version.

Add cream cheese frosting for the stable-inn version.

Copious Simmered Fruit

Inspired by: Any 4 different Fruit
Real-world version: big mixed fruit compote
Serves: /serves:** 6 · Time: 25 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 6 cups mixed fruit
  • 3 tbsp honey or sugar
  • 1/4 cup water or juice
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Pinch salt

Method

  4. Serve warm or chilled.

Egg Pudding

Inspired by: egg, milk, sugar
Real-world version: baked vanilla custard
Serves: 4 · Time: 50 min plus chilling · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: caramel sauce

Method

  1. Heat oven to 325°F / 160°C.
  2. Warm milk until steaming, not boiling.
  3. Whisk eggs, sugar, vanilla, and salt. Slowly whisk in warm milk.
  4. Pour into ramekins and bake in a hot-water bath 30–35 minutes.
  5. Chill and serve with caramel.

Keep this one in the back pocket.

Egg Tart

Inspired by: egg, wheat, sugar, butter
Real-world version: simple custard tartlets
Serves: 8 tartlets · Time: 1 hr · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet pie dough or puff pastry
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Heat oven to 375°F / 190°C.
  2. Cut pastry into muffin tin rounds and press into cups.
  3. Whisk eggs, sugar, milk, cream, vanilla, and salt.
  4. Fill pastry shells three-quarters full.
  5. Bake 22–28 minutes, until custard is set and pastry is golden.

Worth making once and then again by memory.

Energizing Honey Candy

Inspired by: courser bee honey
Real-world version: honey-lemon hard candy
Serves: about 30 pieces · Time: 30 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of salt
  • Powdered sugar for dusting

Method

  1. Line a tray with parchment and dust lightly with powdered sugar.
  2. Boil sugar, honey, water, lemon, and salt to 300°F / 149°C.
  3. Pour carefully onto the tray.
  4. Cool, crack into pieces, and dust to prevent sticking.

Dust well or you'll be prying it off the tray.

Energizing Honey Crepe

Inspired by: milk, egg, wheat, sugar, honey
Real-world version: honey breakfast crepes
Serves: 8 crepes · Time: 35 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • Butter for the pan
  • Honey for serving

Method

  1. Blend flour, eggs, milk, sugar, and salt until smooth.
  2. Rest batter 10 minutes.
  3. Cook thin crepes in a buttered nonstick pan.
  4. Fold and drizzle generously with honey.

Tastes like a longer trip than it took.

Fried Bananas

Inspired by: mighty bananas
Real-world version: caramelized skillet bananas
Serves: 2 · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 bananas, split lengthwise
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • Pinch cinnamon
  • Pinch salt
  • Optional: yogurt or ice cream

Method

  1. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add bananas cut-side down and sprinkle with sugar, cinnamon, and salt.
  3. Cook 2–3 minutes until caramelized.
  4. Serve immediately.

A good one to know by heart.

Fruit Pie

Inspired by: fruit, wheat, butter, sugar
Real-world version: flexible berry-or-stone-fruit pie
Serves: 1 pie · Time: 1 hr 20 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 double pie crust
  • 5 cups sliced fruit or berries
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch or flour
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 egg, beaten

Method

  1. Heat oven to 400°F / 200°C.
  2. Toss fruit with sugar, starch, lemon, and salt.
  3. Fill crust, top with second crust, crimp, vent, and brush with egg.
  4. Bake 45–55 minutes until bubbling.

Whatever fruit is in season — it never disappoints.

Fruitcake

Inspired by: fruit, wheat, sugar
Real-world version: bright tea fruitcake, not the scary holiday brick
Serves: 1 loaf · Time: 1 hr 15 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 cup chopped dried or fresh fruit
  • 1 tsp citrus zest

Method

  1. Heat oven to 350°F / 175°C and line a loaf pan.
  2. Cream butter and sugar, then beat in eggs.
  3. Add dry ingredients alternating with milk.
  4. Fold in fruit and zest.
  5. Bake 45–55 minutes.

Rustic and unfussy — the way camp food should be.

Monster Cake

Inspired by: wheat, sugar, butter, monster extract
Real-world version: black cocoa spice cake with berry “monster extract” glaze
Serves: 8 slices · Time: 1 hr · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup black cocoa or dark cocoa
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup hot coffee or hot water
  • 1/2 cup berry jam, warmed, for glaze

Method

  1. Heat oven to 350°F / 175°C. Grease an 8-inch cake pan.
  2. Whisk dry ingredients. Add egg, milk, butter, and hot coffee or water.
  3. Bake 28–34 minutes.
  4. Cool, then glaze with warmed berry jam so it drips dramatically.

Glaze it until it drips dramatically down the sides.

Nutcake

Inspired by: nuts, wheat, sugar, butter
Real-world version: brown-butter nut cake
Serves: 8 slices · Time: 1 hr · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup butter, browned and cooled slightly
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 3/4 cup chopped walnuts, pecans, or hazelnuts

Method

  1. Heat oven to 350°F / 175°C and grease an 8-inch pan.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Mix butter, sugar, eggs, and milk, then fold into dry ingredients.
  4. Stir in nuts and bake 30–35 minutes.

Brown the butter first; it's what makes it taste expensive.
