Clam Chowder

Inspired by: blueshell snail, milk, wheat, butter
Real-world version: coastal clam chowder
Serves: 4 · Time: 40 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cans chopped clams, juices reserved
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups diced potatoes
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup cream or half-and-half
  • Salt, pepper, parsley

Method

  1. Cook onion and celery in butter until soft.
  2. Stir in flour, then add clam juice and enough water or stock to cover potatoes.
  3. Simmer with potatoes until tender, about 15 minutes.
  4. Add clams, milk, and cream. Warm gently without boiling.
  5. Season heavily with pepper and finish with parsley.

Pepper it hard, the way a harbour cook would.

Copious Seafood Skewers

Inspired by: Any 4 different Seafood
Real-world version: mixed seafood skewer platter
Serves: /serves:** 6 · Time: 30 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mixed seafood: shrimp, scallops, salmon, white fish, crab
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season seafood with oil, lemon, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Thread each type onto skewers.
  3. Grill or broil, pulling delicate skewers as soon as done.
  4. Serve as a coastal feast.

Keep this one in the back pocket.

Crab Stir-Fry

Inspired by: crab and spice
Real-world version: spicy garlic crab with vegetables
Serves: 2–3 · Time: 20 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 8 oz crab meat or cooked crab pieces
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp curry powder or chili crisp
  • 1 tsp honey or sugar
  • Lime wedge

Method

  1. Heat oil in a wide skillet. Stir-fry onion and pepper until browned at the edges.
  2. Add garlic and curry powder or chili crisp for 30 seconds.
  3. Add crab, soy sauce, and honey. Toss until just hot.
  4. Finish with lime and serve over rice.

A good one to know by heart.

Creamy Seafood Soup

Inspired by: seafood, radish, milk, salt
Real-world version: gentle fish chowder
Serves: 4 · Time: 35 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb white fish, cut into chunks
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 leek or onion, sliced
  • 2 cups diced potato or daikon
  • 3 cups seafood or vegetable stock
  • 1 cup milk
  • Salt, pepper, dill or parsley

Method

  1. Cook leek in butter until soft.
  2. Add potato or daikon, stock, salt, and pepper. Simmer until tender.
  3. Add fish and cook 4–6 minutes, just until it flakes.
  4. Stir in milk and herbs. Do not boil after adding milk.

A good one to know by heart.

Fish and Mushroom Skewer

Inspired by: fish plus mushrooms
Real-world version: salmon-mushroom skewers with miso butter
Serves: 4 · Time: 30 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb salmon or firm fish, cubed
  • 8 oz mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp softened butter
  • 1 tbsp miso or soy sauce
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Lemon wedges

Method

  1. Mix butter with miso or soy sauce and honey.
  2. Skewer fish and mushrooms alternately.
  3. Broil or grill 8–10 minutes, brushing with miso butter.
  4. Finish with lemon.

Simple, honest, and better than it needs to be.

Fish Pie

Inspired by: wheat, butter, salt, fish
Real-world version: creamy fish pot pie
Serves: 4 · Time: 1 hr · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 pie crust or puff pastry sheet
  • 1 lb white fish, chunks
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup stock
  • 1 cup peas or chopped greens
  • Salt, pepper, dill

Method

  1. Heat oven to 400°F / 200°C.
  2. Cook onion in butter, stir in flour, then whisk in milk and stock.
  3. Add fish, peas, dill, salt, and pepper.
  4. Pour into a baking dish, cover with pastry, vent, and bake 25–30 minutes.

Worth making once and then again by memory.

Glazed Seafood

Real-world version: honey-lime glazed salmon or shrimp
Serves: 4 · Time: 20 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb salmon fillets or peeled shrimp
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season seafood with salt and pepper.
  2. Sear in oil until almost cooked.
  3. Add honey, lime, soy sauce, and garlic.
  4. Spoon the glaze over the seafood until shiny and cooked through.

Worth making once and then again by memory.

Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry

Inspired by: gourmet meat and seafood
Real-world version: steak-and-shrimp skillet
Serves: 4 · Time: 25 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 12 oz steak, sliced
  • 12 oz shrimp or firm fish
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt, pepper, parsley

Method

  1. Sear steak strips in oil; remove to a plate.
  2. Add seafood and cook until just done.
  3. Return steak with butter, garlic, lemon, salt, and pepper.
  4. Toss quickly and finish with parsley.

Tastes like a longer trip than it took.

Meat and Seafood Fry

Inspired by: meat and seafood
Real-world version: surf-and-turf stir-fry
Serves: 4 · Time: 25 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3/4 lb sliced meat
  • 3/4 lb shrimp, fish, or scallops
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tsp honey

Method

  1. Stir-fry meat until browned; remove.
  2. Stir-fry seafood until just cooked; remove.
  3. Cook pepper and garlic, then return meat and seafood.
  4. Add soy and honey and toss until coated.

Worth making once and then again by memory.

Seafood Paella

Inspired by: rice, butter, salt, shellfish, porgy
Real-world version: weeknight seafood paella
Serves: 4–6 · Time: 50 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups short-grain rice
  • 1/2 lb white fish, chunks
  • 1/2 lb shrimp or mussels
  • 3 cups seafood or chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Pinch saffron or turmeric
  • Salt, lemon wedges

Method

  1. Sauté onion in oil, then add garlic, paprika, and saffron or turmeric.
  2. Stir in rice, then stock and salt. Simmer uncovered 15 minutes.
  3. Nestle seafood on top. Cook 8–10 more minutes until rice is tender and seafood is done.
  4. Rest 5 minutes and serve with lemon.

Let the bottom crisp into socarrat — that's the prize.

Steamed Fish

Inspired by: Any Vegetable, Herb, or Flower; Any Seafood
Real-world version: herb-steamed fish
Serves: /serves:** 4 · Time: 25 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb fish fillets
  • 2 cups greens or herbs
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Lay greens in a steamer or covered skillet.
  2. Place seasoned fish on top.
  3. Steam 8–12 minutes until fish flakes.
  4. Finish with oil and lemon.

Simple, honest, and better than it needs to be.
