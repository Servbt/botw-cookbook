How to use this book

Every recipe here begins with the in-game dish that inspired it and the real-world version you can actually cook. Amounts are written for a home kitchen: taste as you go and adjust. Salt, pepper, and fat (butter or oil) are assumed; where a recipe lists them, they are worth getting right.

Difficulty runs Easy, Medium, or Hard. Times are honest but approximate, and soups and stews are always better the next day.

Carrot Stew

Inspired by: carrot, milk, butter, wheat
Real-world version: creamy carrot-potato stew
Serves: 4 · Time: 45 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb carrots, sliced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 cups vegetable or chicken stock
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1 small potato, diced
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Melt butter in a pot. Cook onion 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in flour for 1 minute, then slowly whisk in stock.
  3. Add carrots, potato, thyme, salt, and pepper. Simmer 25 minutes.
  4. Stir in milk and simmer 5 more minutes.
  5. Mash a few carrots against the pot to thicken; serve with bread.

This simple stew sat simmering for a long time to bring out the sweetness of the carrots.

Cream of Mushroom Soup

Inspired by: mushroom, milk, salt, herbs
Real-world version: rustic creamy mushroom soup
Serves: 4 · Time: 35 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 cups stock
  • 1 cup milk or cream
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown mushrooms in butter until their liquid cooks off.
  2. Add onion, garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper; cook 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in flour, then slowly add stock.
  4. Simmer 15 minutes, then add milk or cream.
  5. Blend part of the soup if you want it thicker.

The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!

Cream of Vegetable Soup

Inspired by: vegetables, milk, salt
Real-world version: creamy garden vegetable soup
Serves: 4 · Time: 35 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 cups chopped vegetables, such as zucchini, greens, peas, or squash
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 cup milk
  • Salt, pepper, and parsley

Method

  1. Cook onion and carrots in butter until softened.
  2. Add remaining vegetables and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Stir in flour, then stock. Simmer until vegetables are tender.
  4. Add milk and warm gently.
  5. Season and finish with parsley.

Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.

Ingredients

Creamy Meat Soup

Inspired by: meat, radish, milk, salt
Real-world version: creamy beef and root vegetable soup
Serves: 4 · Time: 1 hr · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3/4 lb stew beef or ground beef
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cups diced radish, turnip, or potato
  • 3 cups beef stock
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp flour mixed with 2 tbsp water

Method

  1. Brown beef in oil, then add onion and cook until soft.

  3. Simmer 35–40 minutes, until tender.
  4. Stir in milk and flour slurry. Simmer until lightly thickened.

This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.

Inspired by: Salt Pile, Raw Rabbit Leg
Real-world version: seared game cut
Serves: 2–4 · Time: 20–60 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Kosher salt
  • Raw rabbit leg
  • Butter or oil

Method

  1. Marinate the rabbit in oil, garlic, and herbs for a few hours.
  2. Sear in a hot pan, then lower the heat to cook through gently.
  3. Baste with butter and add a splash of stock to keep it moist.
  4. Rest briefly and serve with the pan sauce.

Lean, so keep it moist with butter and a quick hand.

Roasted Tomato Crab Bisque

Inspired by: Salt Pile, Tomato, Butter, Crab Meat
Real-world version: creamy crab bisque
Serves: 2–4 · Time: 20–60 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Kosher salt
  • Tomato
  • Butter
  • Crab meat

Method

  1. Roast the tomatoes with oil and salt until sweet and blistered.
  2. Cook butter and flour into a roux, then whisk in the roasted tomatoes.
  3. Add stock and simmer until thickened, then blend smooth.
  4. Fold in picked crab meat and season; serve with buttered bread.

Roasting the tomatoes is what makes this sing.

Salmon Steak

Inspired by: Salt Pile, Salmon Meat or Salmon
Real-world version: pan-seared steak
Serves: 2–4 · Time: 20–60 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Kosher salt
  • Salmon meat or salmon
  • Butter or oil

Method

  1. Season the steak and let it sit 10 minutes.
  2. Sear skin-side down in hot oil until the skin crisps.
  3. Turn and cook to just under your preferred doneness.
  4. Rest briefly and finish with lemon and butter.

Make sure the skin crisps before you turn it.

Steamed Mudcrab Legs

Real-world version: buttered crab legs
Serves: 2–4 · Time: 20–60 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Crab legs
  • Butter
  • Butter or oil
  • Kosher salt

Method

  1. Steam the legs over salted water with a bay leaf, 8 minutes.
  2. Melt butter with garlic and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Crack the legs and toss in the butter.
  4. Serve hot with extra butter for dipping.

The most direct route from crab to butter to you.

Tomato Soup

Inspired by: Tomato, Salt Pile, Garlic, Leek
Real-world version: simple tavern soup
Serves: 2–4 · Time: 20–60 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Tomato
  • Kosher salt
  • Garlic
  • Leek

Method

  1. Soften garlic and leek in butter without colouring.
  2. Add chopped tomato and stock; simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Blend smooth and season, adding a pinch of sugar if sharp.
  4. Finish with a swirl of cream or a knob of butter.

A tavern staple; a pinch of sugar balances the acid.

Method

Whatever the garden gave you, simmered into a bowl.

Venison Chop

Inspired by: Salt Pile, Venison
Real-world version: seared game cut
Serves: 2–4 · Time: 20–60 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Kosher salt
  • Venison, beef, or lamb
  • Butter or oil

Method

  1. Bring the chop to room temperature and season boldly.
  2. Sear in a hot pan, basting with butter and thyme.
  3. Cook to medium-rare, then rest on a warm plate.
  4. Slice and spoon over the rested pan juices.

Serve it pink; overcooking venison is a small tragedy.

Venison Stew

Inspired by: Venison, Salt Pile, Potato, Leek
Real-world version: Nord-style hearth stew
Serves: 2–4 · Time: 20–60 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Venison, beef, or lamb
  • Kosher salt
  • Potato
  • Leek

Method

  1. Brown the venison hard, then set aside.
  2. Sweat leek and potato, then deglaze with a little ale.
  3. Return the meat with stock and simmer until tender.
  4. Season deeply and serve with a hunk of bread.

The stew that tastes like coming in from the snow.
