Carrot Stew

Inspired by: carrot, milk, butter, wheat
Real-world version: creamy carrot-potato stew
Serves: 4 · Time: 45 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb carrots, sliced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 cups vegetable or chicken stock
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1 small potato, diced
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Melt butter in a pot. Cook onion 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in flour for 1 minute, then slowly whisk in stock.
  3. Add carrots, potato, thyme, salt, and pepper. Simmer 25 minutes.
  4. Stir in milk and simmer 5 more minutes.
  5. Mash a few carrots against the pot to thicken; serve with bread.

This simple stew sat simmering for a long time to bring out the sweetness of the carrots.

Cream of Mushroom Soup

Inspired by: mushroom, milk, salt, herbs
Real-world version: rustic creamy mushroom soup
Serves: 4 · Time: 35 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 cups stock
  • 1 cup milk or cream
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown mushrooms in butter until their liquid cooks off.
  2. Add onion, garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper; cook 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in flour, then slowly add stock.
  4. Simmer 15 minutes, then add milk or cream.
  5. Blend part of the soup if you want it thicker.

The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!

Cream of Vegetable Soup

Inspired by: vegetables, milk, salt
Real-world version: creamy garden vegetable soup
Serves: 4 · Time: 35 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 cups chopped vegetables, such as zucchini, greens, peas, or squash
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 cup milk
  • Salt, pepper, and parsley

Method

  1. Cook onion and carrots in butter until softened.
  2. Add remaining vegetables and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Stir in flour, then stock. Simmer until vegetables are tender.
  4. Add milk and warm gently.
  5. Season and finish with parsley.

Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.

Creamy Heart Soup

Inspired by: radish, hydromelon, voltfruit, milk
Real-world version: chilled melon-radish soup with yogurt
Serves: 4 small bowls · Time: 15 min plus chilling · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3 cups ripe melon, cubed
  • 4 radishes, chopped
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Pinch of salt
  • Mint or basil

Method

  1. Blend melon, radish, yogurt, honey, lime, and salt until smooth.
  2. Chill at least 30 minutes.
  3. Serve cold with torn mint or basil.
  4. For a sweeter dessert version, add more honey and skip half the radish.

Enjoying this sweet soup with another person will bring you both closer together.

Creamy Meat Soup

Inspired by: meat, radish, milk, salt
Real-world version: creamy beef and root vegetable soup
Serves: 4 · Time: 1 hr · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3/4 lb stew beef or ground beef
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cups diced radish, turnip, or potato
  • 3 cups beef stock
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp flour mixed with 2 tbsp water
  • Salt, pepper, thyme

Method

  1. Brown beef in oil, then add onion and cook until soft.
  2. Add root vegetables, stock, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  3. Simmer 35–40 minutes, until tender.
  4. Stir in milk and flour slurry. Simmer until lightly thickened.

This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.

Gourmet Meat Stew

Inspired by: gourmet meat, wheat, milk, butter
Real-world version: creamy beef stew
Serves: 4–6 · Time: 1 hr 45 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb beef stew meat
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 cups beef stock
  • 1 cup milk or cream
  • 2 carrots, chopped

Method

  1. Brown beef in butter, then add onion.

Meat Pie

Inspired by: wheat, butter, salt, meat
Real-world version: savory hand-pie filling in a crust
Serves: 4–6 · Time: 1 hr · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 pie crust or puff pastry
  • 1 lb ground beef or lamb
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1/2 cup stock
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt, pepper, thyme

Method

  1. Brown meat with onion and carrot.
  2. Stir in flour, tomato paste, stock, salt, pepper, and thyme.
  3. Simmer until thick, then cool slightly.
  4. Fill pastry, crimp, vent, and bake at 400°F / 200°C for 25–30 minutes.

You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.

Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin

Inspired by: fortified pumpkin and meat
Real-world version: baked stuffed squash
Serves: 4 · Time: 1 hr 15 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 small pumpkins or acorn squash, halved and seeded
  • 1 lb ground beef, turkey, or sausage
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 1 cup cooked rice or bread cubes
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese, optional
  • Salt, pepper, sage

Method

  1. Roast pumpkin halves cut-side down at 400°F / 200°C for 30 minutes.
  2. Brown meat with onion, sage, salt, and pepper.
  3. Stir in rice or bread cubes and optional cheese.
  4. Fill pumpkins and bake 15–20 more minutes.

This hollow, meat-filled fortified pumpkin is a local specialty or Kakariko Village.

Mushroom Omelet

Inspired by: mushroom, egg, butter, salt
Real-world version: buttery mushroom omelet
Serves: 1–2 · Time: 15 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: cheese or herbs

Method

  1. Brown mushrooms in half the butter with salt and pepper.

Omelet

Inspired by: bird egg
Real-world version: simple soft omelet
Serves: 1 · Time: 8 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2–3 eggs
  • 1 tsp butter
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional herbs or cheese

Method

  1. Beat eggs with salt and pepper.
  2. Melt butter in a nonstick pan over medium-low heat.
  3. Add eggs and stir gently until curds form.
  4. Fold while still soft and glossy.

This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.

Rock-Hard Food

Inspired by: Wood or ore
Real-world version: edible “rock-hard” seed crackers
Serves: /serves:** 1 tray · Time: 45 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole wheat flour
  • 1/2 cup mixed seeds
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/3 cup water
  • Optional cracked pepper

Method

  1. Heat oven to 350°F / 175°C.
  2. Mix ingredients into a stiff dough.
  3. Roll very thin and score into shards.
  4. Bake 25–30 minutes until hard and crisp.

A dish gone awry after adding the wrong ingredient. Chewing your way through this won't be fun, but it will fill you up when you're between a rock and a hard place.

Wheat Bread

Inspired by: Tabantha Wheat; Rock Salt
Real-world version: simple hearth wheat bread
Serves: /serves:** 1 loaf · Time: 2 hr 30 min · Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp yeast
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Mix ingredients into a dough.
  2. Knead until smooth, then rise until doubled.
  3. Shape into a loaf and rise again.
  4. Bake at 400°F / 200°C for 30–35 minutes.

Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.
