Chapter II — Road Food: Skewers, Roasts & Grills

Skewers, roasts, and glazed things cooked over an open fire.

Copious Fried Wild Greens

Inspired by: a full bundle of mixed greens
Real-world version: big skillet greens with herbs and seeds
Serves: 4–6 · Time: 20 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 large bunches mixed greens
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1/4 cup chopped herbs
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame or pumpkin seeds
  • Salt, pepper, lemon

Method

  1. Cook garlic in oil until fragrant.
  2. Add greens in batches until wilted.
  3. Stir in herbs, seeds, salt, and pepper.
  4. Finish with lemon and serve piled high.

A good one to know by heart.

Copious Meat Skewers

Inspired by: four different meats
Real-world version: mixed grill skewers
Serves: 6 · Time: 35 min plus marinade · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mixed meats: chicken, beef, pork, sausage, or lamb
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Cut meats into even pieces and marinate with oil, lemon, garlic, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Thread onto skewers, keeping similar meats together if cooking times differ.
  3. Grill or broil until each skewer is cooked through.
  4. Serve as a feast platter.

Worth making once and then again by memory.

Copious Mushroom Skewers

Inspired by: four different mushrooms
Real-world version: mixed mushroom grill skewers
Serves: 4 · Time: 25 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb mixed mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 garlic clove, grated

Simple, honest, and better than it needs to be.

Fried Wild Greens

Inspired by: wild herbs and greens
Real-world version: garlic sautéed greens
Serves: 4 · Time: 15 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 large bunch kale, chard, spinach, or mixed greens
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced
  • Pinch chili flakes
  • Salt
  • Lemon juice

Method

  1. Heat oil and toast garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add greens by handfuls and toss until wilted.
  3. Season with salt and chili flakes.
  4. Finish with lemon.

Fast, sharp, and better than a vitamin pill.

Glazed Meat

Inspired by: courser bee honey and meat
Real-world version: honey-garlic skillet meat
Serves: 4 · Time: 25 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, pork strips, or beef strips
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar or lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season meat with salt and pepper.
  2. Brown in oil until nearly cooked through.
  3. Add honey, soy sauce, vinegar, and garlic.
  4. Simmer and toss until the sauce turns sticky and coats the meat.
  5. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

Keep this one in the back pocket.

Glazed Mushrooms

Inspired by: courser bee honey and mushrooms
Real-world version: honey-soy glazed mushrooms
Serves: 3–4 · Time: 20 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb mushrooms, halved
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar or lemon juice
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Brown mushrooms in oil until their moisture cooks off.
  2. Add butter, honey, soy sauce, vinegar, and pepper.
  3. Cook 2–3 minutes until glossy and sticky.
  4. Serve over rice or beside roasted meat.

Tastes like a longer trip than it took.

Glazed Veggies

Inspired by: courser bee honey and vegetables
Real-world version: honey-butter glazed carrots and greens
Serves: 4 · Time: 25 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb carrots, squash, green beans, or mixed vegetables
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp water
  • Salt and pepper
  • Lemon juice or chopped herbs

Method

  1. Put vegetables, butter, honey, water, salt, and pepper in a skillet.
  2. Cover and cook until just tender.
  3. Uncover and cook until the liquid reduces to a glaze.
  4. Finish with lemon or herbs.

Worth making once and then again by memory.

Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer

Inspired by: gourmet meat and Goron spice
Real-world version: curry-spiced steak skewers
Serves: 4 · Time: 30 min plus marinade · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb steak or lamb, cubed
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt

Method

  1. Marinate meat with oil, spices, garlic, lemon, and salt for 30 minutes.
  2. Thread onto skewers.
  3. Grill or broil 8–12 minutes, turning often.
  4. Rest before serving.

Worth making once and then again by memory.

Meat Skewer

Inspired by: simple cooked meat
Real-world version: campfire beef skewers
Serves: 4 · Time: 30 min plus marinade · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb beef sirloin or chicken thigh, cubed
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Marinate meat with all seasonings for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Thread onto skewers.
  3. Grill or broil 8–12 minutes, turning often, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Rest 5 minutes before eating.

Keep this one in the back pocket.

Mushroom Skewer

Inspired by: mushroom-only cooking pot meals
Real-world version: garlic-soy grilled mushrooms
Serves: 4 · Time: 25 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb mixed mushrooms, large pieces
  • 2 tbsp melted butter or oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Toss mushrooms with butter, soy sauce, garlic, lemon, and pepper.
  2. Thread onto skewers or spread on a hot sheet pan.
  3. Grill or roast at 425°F / 220°C for 15–18 minutes.
  4. Finish with more lemon.

Rustic and unfussy — the way camp food should be.

Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat

Inspired by: Rock Salt; Any Gourmet Meat
Real-world version: salt-grilled steak
Serves: /serves:** 2 · Time: 20 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 large steak or 2 chops
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Optional butter

Method

  1. Salt meat at least 20 minutes ahead if possible.
  2. Sear or grill over high heat.
  3. Rest 5–10 minutes.
  4. Finish with pepper and optional butter.

A good one to know by heart.
