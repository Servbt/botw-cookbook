Chapter VI — Elixirs & Tonics

Elixirs reborn as real drinks and mocktails.

Chilly Elixir

Inspired by: cooling critter + monster part
Real-world version: cucumber-mint lime cooler
Serves: 2 drinks · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber, chopped
  • 10 mint leaves
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1–2 tbsp honey or simple syrup
  • Sparkling water
  • Ice
  • Optional: pinch of salt

Method

  1. Blend cucumber, mint, lime, honey, and salt.
  2. Strain into two ice-filled glasses.
  3. Top with sparkling water.
  4. Drink before crossing any desert, real or emotional.

Grants a low/high-level cooling effect, raising your body's resistance to heat. Crucial for long journeys through the desert.

Electro Elixir

Inspired by: Electro Critter; Monster Part
Real-world version: ginger-lemon electrolyte sparkler
Serves: /serves:** 2 drinks · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups coconut water
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Pinch of salt
  • Sparkling water and ice

Method

  1. Stir coconut water, lemon, ginger, honey, and salt until dissolved.
  2. Pour over ice.
  3. Top with sparkling water.
  4. Serve when the weather feels electric.

Grants a low/mid/high-level resistance to electricity. Useful against enemies with electrical attacks.

Enduring Elixir

Inspired by: Enduring Critter; Monster Part
Real-world version: banana-oat stamina smoothie
Serves: /serves:** 2 smoothies · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 banana
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 1/2 cups milk or oat milk
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Pinch of salt
  • Ice

Method

  1. Blend everything until smooth.
  2. Add more milk if it is too thick.
  3. Serve cold before a long walk, climb, or work session.

Restores stamina and temporarily extends your Stamina Wheel. The additional stamina will disappear as it's used.

Energizing Elixir

Inspired by: Energizing Critter; Monster Part
Real-world version: citrus honey energy cooler
Serves: /serves:** 2 drinks · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup orange juice
  • Juice of 1 lemon or lime
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 cup cold green tea or mint tea
  • Pinch of salt
  • Ice

Method

  1. Brew and chill the tea if needed.
  2. Shake juice, citrus, honey, tea, and salt with ice.
  3. Pour into glasses.
  4. Add sparkling water if you want it lighter.

Restores your stamina, which is used when performing physical actions such as climbing walls or swimming.

Fairy Tonic

Inspired by: Fairy
Real-world version: rose-berry cream soda
Serves: /serves:** 2 drinks · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup raspberries or strawberries
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/4 tsp rosewater or vanilla
  • Sparkling water
  • Splash of cream or coconut milk
  • Ice

Method

  1. Muddle berries with honey and lemon.
  2. Add rosewater or vanilla.
  3. Pour over ice and top with sparkling water.
  4. Add a small splash of cream for a glowing pink tonic.

This powerful recovery elixir harnesses the power of fairies. It has a sweet fragrance.

Fireproof Elixir

Inspired by: Fireproof Critter; Monster Part
Real-world version: smoky cooling buttermilk lassi
Serves: /serves:** 2 drinks · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain yogurt or buttermilk
  • 1 cup cold water
  • 1/2 cucumber, grated or chopped
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Pinch of smoked paprika
  • Pinch of cumin
  • Salt

Method

  1. Blend yogurt, water, cucumber, lime, spices, and salt.
  2. Chill or pour over ice.
  3. Taste and adjust salt and lime.
  4. Serve with spicy food or hot-weather meals.

Grants a fireproof effect, which prevents your body from catching fire. Be sure to pack this when venturing out to explore Death Mountain.

Hasty Elixir

Inspired by: Hasty Critter; Monster Part
Real-world version: matcha-lime sprint tonic
Serves: /serves:** 2 drinks · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp matcha powder
  • 2 tbsp warm water
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 cups cold sparkling water
  • Mint and ice

Method

  1. Whisk matcha with warm water until smooth.
  2. Stir in honey and lime.
  3. Pour over ice and top with sparkling water.
  4. Slap mint leaves between your palms and add them to the glass.

Grants a low/mid/high-level haste effect, which boosts your movement speed while running, swimming, or climbing.

Hearty Elixir

Inspired by: Hearty Animal; Monster Part
Real-world version: avocado-mango recovery smoothie
Serves: /serves:** 2 smoothies · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe mango or 1 cup frozen mango
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 1 cup orange juice
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Blend all ingredients until creamy.
  2. Add water or juice to thin if needed.
  3. Serve cold as a filling recovery drink.

Restores you to full health and increases your maximum hearts. The additional hearts are lost as you take damage.

Mighty Elixir

Inspired by: Mighty Animal; Monster Part
Real-world version: pomegranate beet power tonic
Serves: /serves:** 2 drinks · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pomegranate juice
  • 1/2 cup beet juice or grated cooked beet
  • Juice of 1 orange
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Pinch of salt
  • Black pepper or ginger, optional

Method

  1. Shake or blend all ingredients.
  2. Strain if using grated beet and you want a smoother drink.
  3. Serve over ice.
  4. Add ginger for extra bite.

Grants a low/mid/high-level might effect, which strengthens your body and mind to boost your attack power with all weapons.

Sneaky Elixir

Inspired by: Sneaky Critter; Monster Part
Real-world version: lavender-chamomile night tea
Serves: /serves:** 2 cups · Time: 10 min plus steeping · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups hot water
  • 2 chamomile tea bags or 2 tsp loose chamomile
  • 1 tsp dried lavender or a tiny drop lavender extract
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Lemon slice

Method

  1. Steep chamomile and lavender in hot water for 5 minutes.
  2. Strain if needed.
  3. Stir in honey.
  4. Serve warm and quiet.

Grants a low/mid/high-level stealth effect, which calms the nerves and silences footfalls. Allows you to move about undetected by monsters and animals.

Spicy Elixir

Inspired by: Spicy Critter; Monster Part
Real-world version: hot ginger apple warmer
Serves: /serves:** 2 mugs · Time: 15 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups apple cider or apple juice
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Pinch cayenne or chili flakes
  • Honey to taste

Method

  1. Simmer cider with ginger and cinnamon for 10 minutes.
  2. Add lemon and a tiny pinch of cayenne.
  3. Sweeten to taste.
  4. Serve hot before cold-weather chapters.

Warms your body from its core, increasing your resistance to cold environments. Very useful in the snow-covered mountains.

Tough Elixir

Inspired by: Tough Critter; Monster Part
Real-world version: turmeric carrot defense tonic
Serves: /serves:** 2 drinks · Time: 10 min · Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup carrot juice
  • 1 cup orange juice
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of black pepper
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Shake or blend all ingredients.
  2. Pour over ice, or warm gently for a hot version.
  3. Taste and adjust lemon and salt.
  4. Serve bright orange and bracing.

Grants a low/mid/high-level toughness effect, which fortifies your bones to strengthen your defense. Best to use before facing off against hard-hitting enemies.
