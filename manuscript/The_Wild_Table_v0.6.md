## How to Use This Book

Every recipe gives the in-game dish that inspired it and a real-world version you can actually cook.
Difficulty is Easy / Medium / Hard; times are approximate. Salt, pepper, and fat are assumed.

## Pantry Translation Guide

- **Tabantha Wheat:** all-purpose flour, bread flour, pie crust, pasta, or cooked wheat berries
- **Hylian Rice:** short-grain rice, sushi rice, arborio, jasmine, or calrose
- **Goat Butter:** butter; goat butter if available
- **Fresh Milk:** whole milk; oat milk for dairy-free versions
- **Rock Salt / Salt Pile:** kosher salt or flaky sea salt
- **Goron Spice:** Japanese curry powder, garam masala, smoked paprika, chili, or a house curry blend
- **Monster Extract / Dark Clump:** berry reduction, black garlic molasses, squid ink, balsamic glaze, or dark spice syrup

## Conversions

- 1 cup flour ≈ 120 g · 1 cup sugar ≈ 200 g · 1 cup rice ≈ 190 g
- 1 tbsp ≈ 15 ml · 1 tsp ≈ 5 ml · 1 stick butter ≈ 113 g
- 350°F = 175°C · 375°F = 190°C · 400°F = 200°C · 425°F = 220°C

### Carrot Stew
**Inspired by:** carrot, milk, butter, wheat  
**Real-world version:** creamy carrot-potato stew  
**Serves:** 4 | **Time:** 45 min | **Difficulty:** Easy  
**Tags:** stable-comforts, easy

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb carrots, sliced
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp flour
- 3 cups vegetable or chicken stock
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1 small potato, diced
- 1 tsp thyme
- Salt and pepper

**Method**
1. Melt butter in a pot. Cook onion 5 minutes.
2. Stir in flour for 1 minute, then slowly whisk in stock.
3. Add carrots, potato, thyme, salt, and pepper. Simmer 25 minutes.
4. Stir in milk and simmer 5 more minutes.
5. Mash a few carrots against the pot to thicken; serve with bread.

**Serving note:** This simple stew sat simmering for a long time to bring out the sweetness of the carrots.

### Cream of Mushroom Soup
**Inspired by:** mushroom, milk, salt, herbs  
**Real-world version:** rustic creamy mushroom soup  
**Serves:** 4 | **Time:** 35 min | **Difficulty:** Easy  
**Tags:** stable-comforts, vegetarian, easy

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb mushrooms, sliced
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp flour
- 3 cups stock
- 1 cup milk or cream
- 1 tsp thyme
- Salt and pepper

**Method**
1. Brown mushrooms in butter until their liquid cooks off.
2. Add onion, garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper; cook 5 minutes.
3. Stir in flour, then slowly add stock.
4. Simmer 15 minutes, then add milk or cream.
5. Blend part of the soup if you want it thicker.

**Serving note:** The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!

### Cream of Vegetable Soup
**Inspired by:** vegetables, milk, salt  
**Real-world version:** creamy garden vegetable soup  
**Serves:** 4 | **Time:** 35 min | **Difficulty:** Easy  
**Tags:** stable-comforts, vegetarian, easy

**Ingredients**
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 cups chopped vegetables, such as zucchini, greens, peas, or squash
- 2 tbsp flour
- 3 cups vegetable stock
- 1 cup milk
- Salt, pepper, and parsley

**Method**
1. Cook onion and carrots in butter until softened.
2. Add remaining vegetables and cook 3 minutes.
3. Stir in flour, then stock. Simmer until vegetables are tender.
4. Add milk and warm gently.
5. Season and finish with parsley.

**Serving note:** Made by simmering vegetables in milk, this healthy dish is as simple as the ingredients.

**Inspired by:** radish, hydromelon, voltfruit, milk

**Serves:** 4 small bowls | **Time:** 15 min plus chilling | **Difficulty:** Easy

### Creamy Meat Soup
**Inspired by:** meat, radish, milk, salt  
**Real-world version:** creamy beef and root vegetable soup  
**Serves:** 4 | **Time:** 1 hr | **Difficulty:** Easy  
**Tags:** stable-comforts, easy

**Ingredients**
- 3/4 lb stew beef or ground beef
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cups diced radish, turnip, or potato
- 3 cups beef stock
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tbsp flour mixed with 2 tbsp water
- Salt, pepper, thyme

**Method**
1. Brown beef in oil, then add onion and cook until soft.
2. Add root vegetables, stock, thyme, salt, and pepper.
3. Simmer 35–40 minutes, until tender.
4. Stir in milk and flour slurry. Simmer until lightly thickened.

**Serving note:** This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.

### Crab Risotto
**Inspired by:** rice, butter, rock salt, crab  
**Real-world version:** lemon-butter crab risotto  
**Serves:** 4 | **Time:** 35 min | **Difficulty:** Medium  
**Tags:** rice-curry-bowls, gluten-free

**Ingredients**

- 6 oz crab meat

- 2 tbsp butter, plus 1 tbsp to finish
- 1 shallot, minced
- 1/2 cup white wine or extra stock
- 1/4 cup parmesan
- Lemon zest, salt, pepper

**Serving note:** An everyday staple of seaside villages, the secret to its delicious flavor lies in crab fat.
